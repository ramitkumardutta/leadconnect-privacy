# LeadConnect PoC – Privacy Policy **Last updated:** November 9, 2026 ## 1. About this project LeadConnect PoC is an academic proof-of-concept project demonstrating real-time lead retrieval and management using Meta Lead Ads, Node.js and React Native. ## 2. Information we collect When you submit our test lead form, we may receive: - Full name - Email address - Phone number - Company name - City - Job title ## 3. How information is used The information is used solely to demonstrate the functionality of the LeadConnect PoC, including retrieving lead submissions through Meta's APIs and displaying them in the LeadConnect mobile application. ## 4. Data handling Information is transmitted to the project's backend for processing and displayed in the connected application. During development, technical logs may also contain submitted lead information. This project is a demonstration and is not intended for submitting sensitive or confidential personal information. ## 5. Sharing of information We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Meta as part of its lead form and platform services. ## 6. Data retention and deletion This proof of concept does not currently provide a permanent customer database. Test information may be temporarily processed or appear in development logs. Please contact the project owner to request deletion of information under the project's control. ## 7.
